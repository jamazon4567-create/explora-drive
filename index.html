Explora Drive

A personal integration that connects its owner's Google Drive to software running on the owner's computers and private server.

It uses rclone and Google Drive authorization to read, create and update files for personal workflows, including private planning documents and health-analysis exports. It is operated solely by its owner and is not a service offered to the public.

Access and control

The owner authorizes access through Google. The integration requests Drive access needed for its file workflows. Authorization can be revoked through the owner's Google Account.

This public website describes the integration; it does not provide access to the owner's files or accept uploads.

See the privacy policy for data handling details. For questions, use the developer contact email shown on Google's consent screen.
