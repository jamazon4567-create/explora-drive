Explora Drive · Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Explora Drive is a personal-use integration operated by its owner. This policy describes the integration's handling of Google Drive data.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's authorization, the integration accesses file contents and metadata in Google Drive to retrieve, create and update documents used in personal planning and analysis. Selected documents may contain personal or health information. OAuth credentials allow the integration to perform these authorized operations.

Storage and retention

Selected files and derived results may be stored in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computers or private server. Local records may remain after a source file is deleted or access is revoked. The owner manages retention and can delete those copies separately. Credentials are kept in private configuration files and are not published on this website.

Sharing and use

The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Data is processed by Google and the owner's configured tools. When the owner directs an AI-assisted workflow, selected data may also be processed by that configured AI provider under its applicable terms and privacy settings. Private file contents and credentials are not intentionally published to this website or its public source repository.

Explora Drive's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and revocation

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections and remove stored files and credentials from their systems. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete previously retained copies.

This website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, upload forms or application login. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact and changes

Use the developer contact email displayed on Google's consent screen for questions. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated date.
